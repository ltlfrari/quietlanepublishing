About David E. Ellis

David E. Ellis is a writer, technologist, and enthusiastic adopter of artificial intelligence, based in Wake Forest, North Carolina.

His publishing debut, Poems of a Childhood, is a unique collaboration between human imagination and AI creativity. David provided the prompts — many rooted in vivid memories of his own childhood — which were brought to life in verse by Claude AI and given visual form through illustrations by ChatGPT. The result is something that feels both nostalgic and entirely new: the warmth of lived experience, expressed through the most modern of tools.

By day, David works in the world of IBM mainframe computing, with deep expertise in technologies like z/OS, HLASM, and REXX — systems that power much of the world quietly and invisibly. He also writes about technology on his blog at davideellis.wordpress.com, exploring the growing intersection of AI and traditional computing.

He came to creative writing not through a literary background, but through curiosity — a desire to see what these remarkable new AI systems could produce when pointed in the right direction. The answer, it turns out, is something worth reading.
