In this bright and colorful children’s picture book, young readers follow Dan through the changing seasons as he discovers the special joys each one can bring. From flowers and sunshine to falling leaves, chilly days, and cozy moments with friends, Dan explores the world around him with curiosity and cheer.

With simple language, cheerful illustrations, and a warm, gentle tone, Dan D Lion’s Favorite Season is designed especially for toddlers and preschool children. The story encourages early learning about nature, the seasons, friendship, kindness, and noticing the small wonders that make each time of year special.

Perfect for read-aloud story time, bedtime, or quiet moments together, this book offers a sweet introduction to the seasons and the joy of sharing discoveries with friends.

A lovely choice for young children who enjoy animals, flowers, sunshine, changing weather, and gentle picture book adventures.
